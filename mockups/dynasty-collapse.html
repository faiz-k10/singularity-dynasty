The Collapse

One scene, two readings. As a cube, each player floats at his dynasty value, with age running left to right and win-now running front to back; each tier is a floor, and the empty space between floors is a cliff. Collapse it and the same floors fold flat into a ranked tier table, while each player's range bar swings from upright to sideways. Drag the slider to watch it happen.

One rule, three boxes

70%Tied: nobody inside is 70%+ likely to beat anyone else inside.
90%Tier: nobody inside is 90%+ likely to beat anyone else inside.
99.9%Group: the same rule at 99.9%.

The red % on a break is how often a player from the box above ends up worth more than a player from the box below (bold = a group break, comparing the two groups). Every box uses the fewest splits the rule allows, placed at the biggest drops.

Why the cube and the table agree

The table's top-to-bottom order is the cube's height, squeezed into even rows. Because the squeeze never changes order, no two players can swap places at any point in the fold, and floors can never overlap. Each player's 80% value range is the same bar in both views: upright in the cube, sideways in the table.

Reading the cube

Height = dynasty value (5 seasons, age-adjusted, over replacement). Left to right = age. Front to back = win-now (this season). Floors = tiers, tinted by group. The more certain a break, the more space it gets in the table. Filter by position and the tiers are recomputed for just those players.
