    Why this is not gambling
    • Nothing goes in. No money, credits or anything of value is bought, staked or wagered. Stars only come from using the site.
    • Nothing can be bought. No stars, collapses or finds are for sale, at any plan.
    • Every collapse pays. There is no losing outcome.
    • Finds are collectibles and site perks. Anything with cash value (plan time, the printed star chart) comes from orbit milestones, earned by effort, or from Beat the Model, earned by skill. Never from chance.
    • Odds are published, and every find is drawn fairly before the telescope shows it.
