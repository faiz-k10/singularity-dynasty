    Why this is not gambling
    • Nothing goes in. No money, credits or anything of value is bought, staked or wagered. Stars only come from using the site.
    • Nothing can be bought. No stars, collapses or finds are for sale, at any plan.
    • Every collapse pays. There is no losing outcome.
    • Prizes are site perks (cosmetics, early access, short unlocks). Anything with cash value, like a free month, only comes from the orbit milestones, which are earned by effort, not chance.
    • Odds are published and the find is drawn fairly before the telescope shows it.
