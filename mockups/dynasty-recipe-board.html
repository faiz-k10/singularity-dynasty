Dynasty Rankings

Read it like a recipe: player → tied → tier → group

One rule, three boxes

Every box is drawn by the same test on the model's simulations: how often does a player above beat a player below? Tied — under 70%, the order inside is close to a coin flip. Tier — nobody inside beats anyone else inside 90% of the time. Group — the same at 99.9%. Each uses the fewest boxes the rule allows, split at the biggest drops.

The red line

At the foot of each tier: how often a player from that tier ends up worth more than a player from the next one. A heavy rule across the row is a group break; a thinner one is a tier break. The grey bar is each player's 80% value range — overlapping bars are why players share a box.

Filter and it re-fits

Pick a position and the boxes are re-drawn for just those players, so a WR tier is a WR tier, not a slice of the overall one. Switch to Superflex and quarterbacks climb; the tiers re-form around them. Hover a tier to light up its players.
